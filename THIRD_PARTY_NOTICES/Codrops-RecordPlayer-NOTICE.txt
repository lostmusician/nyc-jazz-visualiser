Interactive Record Player reference
===================================

The tonearm circle-intersection geometry, angle-to-playback mapping, and
lift/drag/drop interaction in this project are adapted to React, TypeScript, and
Pointer Events from Codrops' RecordPlayer experiment:

https://github.com/codrops/RecordPlayer

Original main.js copyright 2016 Codrops and distributed under the MIT License.
No visual assets, audio effects, impulse responses, fonts, or bundled recordings
from that project are redistributed here.
